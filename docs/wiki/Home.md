# hold your voice

a draft can sound less generic and still lose the writer. it can also copy a writer's cadence while making empty claims. hold your voice keeps those checks separate.

the local CLI builds a portable VoiceDNA profile from your samples, runs VoiceDNA and AI Editor as independent engines, creates a five-tier rewrite brief, and verifies a candidate. model calls, uploads, accounts, and telemetry stay outside the package.

start with [getting started](Getting-Started). read [core concepts](Core-Concepts) before adding a rule.
