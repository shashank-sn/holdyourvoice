# pattern catalog

the [220-pattern catalog](https://github.com/shashank-sn/holdyourvoice/blob/main/docs/patterns/AI-WRITING-PATTERNS-1-220.md) is editorial guidance. it cannot detect authorship or provide a verdict. it has broad examples and legitimate exceptions that are outside the CLI ruleset.

a catalog item can become executable only after deduplication, a clear definition, counterexamples, public provenance review, a severity decision, and positive and negative tests. keep the catalog and deterministic ruleset separate.
